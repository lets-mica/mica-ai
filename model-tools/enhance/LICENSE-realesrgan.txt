realesr-general-x4v3.onnx — Real-ESRGAN "realesr-general-x4v3" weights
(SRVGGNetCompact, 64 features, 32 convs, 4x), converted to ONNX by
scripts/build-upscaler-model.mjs in the Microapp repository. The weights are
unmodified apart from the container format; the fp16 values are the ones
upstream publishes.

Upstream: https://github.com/xinntao/Real-ESRGAN
Paper:    Xintao Wang, Liangbin Xie, Chao Dong, Ying Shan. "Real-ESRGAN:
          Training Real-World Blind Super-Resolution with Pure Synthetic
          Data." ICCV Workshops, 2021.
